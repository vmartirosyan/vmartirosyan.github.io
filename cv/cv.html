Dr. Vahram Martirosyan

Teaching Philosophy & Profile

Ph.D. in Computational Mathematics with 15+ years of classroom teaching across universities in Armenia and the UAE, combined with 20+ years of professional software engineering and active research. I believe rigorous computer-science education must connect theory to practice: my courses consistently move students from first principles to hands-on implementation using real systems. Current areas of instruction span programming (C, C++, Python), system architecture, data structures & algorithms, operating systems, IoT & security, machine learning & data science, and statistics. Active researcher in intrinsically explainable AI via polynomial regression models, applied to medicine (ECG and clinical diagnosis), materials science, precision agriculture, and ocean/climate science, and in HPC & compiler engineering: LLVM-based automatic parallelisation (HPC++) and binary lifting of legacy code (LEAP). 25+ publications, including accepted and published IEEE-indexed conference papers, several manuscripts under review, and a pending patent, with a track record of integrating research directly into the classroom. Currently Chief Scientist at ExplainableAI.app and Assistant Professor and Course Team Leader (multi-campus) at Higher Colleges of Technology, Abu Dhabi.

    43-program liftability study: 100% at -O0 and 98% at -O1/O2/O3; 3.7× geomean speedup from source IR; uplifted binary 10.3× over -O0 and 2.1× over -O2; 246× peak GPU offload; 377/377 tests pass.
