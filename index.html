Dr. Vahram Martirosyan

Interpretable ML for Ocean Science · Explainable AI · Edge AI · HPC & Compiler Engineering

Doctor of Computational Mathematics with 23+ years across research, industry, and academia. I build intrinsically interpretable polynomial regression models for ocean and climate science, medicine, materials, and remote sensing — and the compilers and runtimes that make them fast, from LLVM auto-parallelisation to sub-millisecond inference on tiny devices. Currently Chief Scientist at ExplainableAI.app and Assistant Professor at Higher Colleges of Technology, Abu Dhabi.
