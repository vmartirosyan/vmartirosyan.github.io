Before you start

A quick guide to Studio

Everything runs locally in your browser. Here is the shortest path from raw data to an explainable model.

1
Bring data

Use a CSV for tabular models or a folder of labelled images for an image classifier. Nothing is uploaded.

2
Choose the target

Studio recommends a target and preselects the model type. For numeric targets, you can switch between regression and classification.

3
Prepare and train

Review the cleaning plan, then train a polynomial model with WASM. The held-out split is scored automatically.

4
Evaluate and explain

Inspect metrics, errors, feature importance, and exact per-prediction contributions.

5
Run and share

Test predictions, check out-of-distribution inputs, share a results kit, or export a standalone app.

Share kit

Show off your model

Images and text are generated locally. Nothing is uploaded until you choose to post.

Uses your system share sheet when it supports file sharing. Pick Facebook there if it is available. Otherwise Studio copies the caption, downloads the images, and opens Facebook for manual attachment.
